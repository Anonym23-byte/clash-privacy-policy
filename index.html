1. Overview & Data We Collect

Clash: ChaosChooser is designed as an offline-first casual decision-maker and entertainment application. We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical location, contacts, or browsing history.

The following data is stored exclusively on your device's local memory (Local Storage / AsyncStorage):

  • Custom Options & Presets: The battle options, food names, or participant lists you input are saved strictly inside your local device.
  • Decision History (Grave of Decisions): Elimination logs and battle results are stored locally and can be cleared at any time via the in-app wipe button.
  • User Preferences: Audio toggles, selected UI language, and color theme preferences (Light / Midnight Dark).

2. Device Hardware Permissions

The app only requests hardware access strictly necessary for core interactive gameplay features:

Hardware / Sensor Intended Purpose Data Handling
Accelerometer / Motion Detects device shake events ("Shake to Chaos") and tilts arena gravity in real-time. Processed in volatile RAM; never logged or transmitted.
Vibration / Haptics Delivers tactile feedback when fighters collide or contestants are eliminated. Zero data collected.
Text-to-Speech (TTS) Generates synthesized voice announcements for battle commentary and winners. Synthesized entirely by your device's built-in speech engine.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Clash is built with an offline-first architecture. If advertisements are delivered through verified mobile ad networks (such as Google AdMob):

  • Users may reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via their device system settings (Android Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Clash contains no hidden tracking SDKs that profile your activity outside the app.
